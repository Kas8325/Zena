Platform

Everything African markets, one terminal

Four integrated layers that give you the full picture of African capital markets — from macro intelligence to micro-level transfer pricing.

01

Markets & News Intelligence

Real-time equity indices (JSE, NGX, NSE), sovereign bonds, commodities, and curated news covering VC, M&A, geopolitics, and policy changes across 12+ African markets.

02

Transfer Compare

Compare fees, exchange rates, and delivery speed across 10+ money transfer providers. See the real spread — not the advertised rate. Save up to 70% on remittance fees.

03

Cross-Currency FX

Trade directly between African currencies (NGN/KES, ZAR/GHS) without the USD intermediary. Forward curves, swap pricing, and tenor analysis powered by PAPSS settlement.

04

Stablecoin Bridge

Compare USDT and USDC off-ramp providers for cheaper diaspora remittances. DeFi rails meeting traditional corridors — the future of Africa payments.

05

Central Bank Watch

Track monetary policy decisions, next rate meetings, and market expectations for 12 African central banks. 1-2 year forward rate projections and probability analysis.

06

Forward & Swap Pricer

Price FX forwards and swaps for any African or global currency pair. Interest rate differential-driven curves, outright rates, and swap points across 7 tenors.
