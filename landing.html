For companies & NGOs

A treasury tool for NGOs

A workspace for the finance team that pays suppliers, staff and programmes across different corridors.

01

Live best-route comparison

Every provider and route for each payment, ranked on all-in cost (exchange spread fee plus cost of sending fee) against the mid-market rate.

02

Rate alerts

Set a target on any corridor. Zena tells you when the best all-in rate gets there, so you pay at the right moment.

03

Dated audit trail

We analyse your past payments, flag the costly ones and recommend the cheapest provider, so you save on the next one.

04

Onboarding made easy

Zena helps you collect the documents needed to onboard with the cheapest provider, and keeps them ready to re-use if you switch providers later.

05

Multi-corridor dashboard

Kenya, Morocco, Senegal, Nigeria, Uganda: every corridor you pay into on one screen, with what you save on each.

06

FX exposure view

What your upcoming payables in shillings, dirhams or naira are worth today, and what a 5% move would cost you.

“We pay around €500 in fees on every €15–20,000 we send to Morocco.” An SME founder paying suppliers in Morocco. That is 2.5–3.3% on every payment.

For individuals

Send money home for less

For the diaspora in Europe, the UK, North America and the Gulf, and for anyone moving money between African countries.

01

The true cost, upfront

“Zero fees” still costs you: the spread is hidden in the rate. Zena shows each provider’s rate next to the mid-market rate, and what your recipient actually gets.

02

Verify once

Every provider asks for your ID, a selfie and proof of address. With Zena you do it once, and can use whichever provider is cheapest that day.

03

Bank, mobile money or crypto

Pay out to a bank account, to M-Pesa, MTN MoMo, Wave and others, or to a stablecoin wallet.

04

A wallet in CHF, USD and EUR

Hold and convert currencies at the mid-market rate with a small fee you can see, and link your card.
