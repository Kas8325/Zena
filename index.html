Every route has up to three costs: buying the stablecoin (on-ramp), moving it on-chain (network fee) and converting it to local currency (off-ramp). If the recipient takes crypto directly, there is no off-ramp. Where the local street rate is above the official rate, that premium can outweigh the fees.
